    How it works: for each area the page adds up recent rain and takes away what has drained and dried, then guesses how the soil will be on each day. It is a guess from weather data, not a trail report. Ride reports from this group move the guesses. Closure and event notices come from the agencies' own pages, a twice-weekly web search, and riders' reports. They can be late or miss things, so no notice does not mean open. Always check the park's own status before you load up: Carnegie, Hollister Hills and Metcalf close trails when wet. Rain history: Iowa Environmental Mesonet (NOAA data). Forecast and snow level: National Weather Service.
